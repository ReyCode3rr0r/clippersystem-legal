ClipperSystem — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026

1. Who this covers

This policy covers the one TikTok account connected to ClipperSystem by its owner. There are no other users, and no data from anyone else is ever collected.

2. What data is accessed

Through TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API: basic profile info (user.info.basic) to confirm the connected account, and video publishing (video.publish) to upload videos the owner created.

3. Where data is stored

TikTok's access tokens are stored in a local file on the owner's own computer. Nothing is uploaded to any server other than TikTok's own API.

4. What is never done with this data

Never sold, shared, or shown to any third party. Never used for advertising or analytics. Never used to access any account other than the owner's own.

5. Contact

This app is operated by a single individual for personal use. Questions can be directed through the developer account associated with this application in the TikTok Developer Portal.
